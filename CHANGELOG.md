## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-09

### Added

- Native Home Assistant Companion app UPC/EAN scanning with manual entry as a
  fallback.
- Product lookup through Open Food Facts.
- Server-side learned barcode catalog shared by users of one Home Assistant
  instance.
- Default integration with `todo.shopping_list` and
  `todo.upc_lookup_catalog`.
- Configurable target list, catalog, display name, country, and language.
